# 2. Decision 004 — Marketplace Philosophy

## Decision

Marketplace is the commercial engine of ClientOS.

ClientOS is free.

Marketplace generates revenue.

Marketplace exists to:

- find suppliers
- compare availability
- compare pricing
- reserve inventory
- transact safely
- automate paperwork

Marketplace is NOT:

a copy of supplier catalogues.

Each supplier maintains their own published inventory.

Everyone searches the same live data.

## Development Impact

Never duplicate supplier inventory inside another ClientOS workspace.
