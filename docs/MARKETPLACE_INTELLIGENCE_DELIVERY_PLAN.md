# Marketplace Intelligence Delivery Plan

## Controlling outcome

EventOS must let a customer or planner describe an event, discover suitable products and suppliers, build preliminary visual concepts and estimates, revise them freely, obtain client approval, and only then convert the approved concept into controlled quotations, contracts, purchase orders, bookings, execution and finance workflows.

This plan is the controlling delivery sequence for the Marketplace experience. Work that does not advance one of the gates below is not a substitute for completing the sequence.

## Frozen product boundaries

- ClientOS is the private source of truth for inventory, availability, pricing, supplier operations and commercial records.
- Marketplace receives only deliberately published customer-facing information.
- Guided supplier entry and all Marketplace discovery paths use the same Event Product Intelligence Model.
- Mood boards, packages and estimates remain preliminary and editable until the customer approves a direction.
- EventOS must not silently reserve stock, accept a supplier quotation, issue a purchase order, sign a contract or confirm a booking.
- AI may interpret, recommend, compose and explain. Human operators retain authority over commercial commitments.

## North-star workflow

1. Capture the event basics: type, date, location, venue position and guest count.
2. Capture theme, look, colours, atmosphere, priorities, budget and flexibility.
3. Interpret the brief into explicit product, service and supplier requirements.
4. Discover compatible published offerings through AI assistance, guided building or manual expert search.
5. Assemble one or more editable visual directions using real published offering imagery and pricing evidence.
6. Produce a preliminary package, cost estimate and customer-facing proposal for each direction.
7. Revise products, quantities, suppliers, visuals and commercial assumptions without losing the brief or prior versions.
8. Record customer approval of a chosen direction.
9. Request and reconcile final supplier quotations, availability and substitutions.
10. Convert approved information into governed contracts, purchase orders, bookings, execution and finance records in ClientOS.

## Delivery gates

### MI-001 — Shared product meaning

Supplier product entry and Marketplace search share the same normalization, concepts, synonyms, visual attributes and relationship rules.

Completion evidence:

- Singular and plural customer language resolves consistently.
- Direct products rank ahead of accessories and transport/storage equipment.
- Supplier-entered attributes enrich the same search concepts used by Marketplace.
- Automated examples cover tables, table runners, chairs, forks, glassware, underplates, ottomans and plinths.

### MI-002 — Measurable discovery quality

A versioned gold-standard evaluation set represents realistic customer searches, themes and event briefs.

Completion evidence:

- Expected product types and exclusions are declared for every evaluation.
- Exact products, related accessories and operational equipment have measurable ranking targets.
- Failures are reported automatically and block regression.
- The set includes simple item searches, ambiguous language, themes, colours, styles and multi-requirement briefs.

### MI-003 — Three converging discovery paths

AI Event Assistant, Guided Event Builder and Manual Expert Search all create and edit the same event concept.

Completion evidence:

- A user can switch paths without losing selections or brief information.
- Combined requirements drive combined recommendations.
- Results explain why they match and expose practical alternatives.
- Users can add, remove, replace and compare suppliers at any stage before approval.

### MI-004 — Visual concept and mood-board workflow

Published supplier imagery can be assembled into versioned event directions with manual editing and optional external-design import.

Completion evidence:

- Multiple directions and revisions can coexist.
- Images retain offering and supplier provenance.
- Layout instructions and manual adjustments are preserved.
- Concepts clearly distinguish preliminary selections from confirmed supply.

### MI-005 — Preliminary package and proposal

Each visual direction produces a traceable estimate and customer-facing proposal before any supplier commitment.

Completion evidence:

- Quantities, prices, assumptions, exclusions and uncertainty are visible.
- Alternatives can be compared without overwriting prior drafts.
- Customer comments, revisions and approval are auditable.
- Approval freezes a version for controlled commercial conversion.

### MI-006 — Governed commercial conversion

An approved concept enters ClientOS quotation, availability, contract, purchase-order, booking, execution and finance workflows.

Completion evidence:

- Final supplier information is reconciled against the approved concept.
- Substitutions and price changes require explicit review.
- Contracts may use EventOS templates or controlled client-imported templates.
- Commercial records remain private and authoritative in ClientOS.

## Delivery discipline

Every implementation package must declare:

- the gate it advances;
- the customer or operator behaviour it enables;
- the authoritative data it reads or writes;
- automated acceptance evidence;
- unresolved risks and the next smallest gate-aligned slice.

Disconnected appearance work, isolated demonstrations and catalogue volume do not complete a gate unless the required behaviour and evidence are present.
